# Rogue - Class Design Document

## 1. Class Fantasy: "The Assassin"
The Rogue is the master of stealth, poisons, and precision strikes. In a group, they strike from behind. In a Solo Server environment, they are the **Dirty Fighters**. They use tricks, stuns, and poisons to create openings, allowing them to land fatal blows even when the enemy is facing them.

*   **Primary Stat:** **Dexterity (DEX)**
*   **Class Mastery:** **"Assassin's Art"**
    *   **Lethal Precision:** Dexterity increases **Critical Hit Damage** (Crit Multiplier).
    *   **Master Poisoner:** Dexterity increases the proc rate and potency of applied poisons.

---

## 2. Combat Profile

### A. Dealing Damage (The Burst)
Rogues deal the highest single-target burst damage in the game.
*   **Primary Source:** **Backstab** (and "Frontstab" mechanics).
*   **Secondary Source:** **Poisons** (Procs for damage, slow, snare).
*   **Mechanic:** **"Dirty Fighting"**. Since solo Rogues cannot always get behind a mob, they gain the **"Gouge"** ability (or a modified Backstab) that works from the front but deals slightly less damage. Alternatively, they use **Stuns** (like *Kidney Smash*) to turn the mob around or incapacitate it long enough to strike a vital spot.

### B. Taking Damage (Avoidance)
Rogues wear Chain/Leather and rely on not getting hit.
*   **Mitigation:** Moderate AC.
*   **Survival:** **Evasion**. High Agility/Defense allows them to dodge attacks.
*   **Panic Button:** **Escape / Vanish**. Instantly drops all aggro and enters Stealth.

---

## 3. The Toolkit

### A. Damage Tools
1.  **Backstab:** Massive damage attack. (Modified to work from front via AA/Disc or "Dirty Fighting" passive).
2.  **Assassinate:** A chance to instantly kill humanoid targets (Level 60+).
3.  **Poisons:**
    *   *Spider's Bite:* Nature Damage Proc.
    *   *Scorpion's Agony:* Threat reduction (useless solo) -> Changed to **Armor Break** (Debuff AC).
4.  **Duelist (Disc):** Increases damage and attack speed.

### B. Mitigation/Control Tools
1.  **Kidney Smash (Stun):** Stuns the target and stops them from attacking/casting.
2.  **Blind:** Lowers enemy accuracy significantly.
3.  **Tumble:** A short-cooldown dodge that avoids the next attack.

### C. Utility
1.  **Stealth/Hide/Sneak:** Move unseen. Essential for dungeon crawling to specific bosses.
2.  **Pick Lock:** Open doors and chests.
3.  **Disarm Traps:** Essential for certain dungeons.
4.  **Safe Fall:** No fall damage.

### D. Area Effect (AE) Potential
*   **Fan of Knives:** Throws daggers in a cone in front of the Rogue.
*   **Poison Bomb:** Explodes a poison vial, applying DoTs to nearby enemies.

---

## 4. Solo Strategy
*   **Level 1-30:** "Frontstab" tanking. Use poisons to debuff.
*   **Level 30-60:** **Stun Locking**. Open from stealth (massive damage), then use Kidney Smash and Blind to mitigate incoming damage while finishing the target.
*   **Level 70+:** **Assassination Runs**. Stealth through a dungeon, find the named mob, Assassinate it. If it fails, Vanish and try again.

## 6. Summary
The Rogue is the **Dungeon Crawler**. You don't kill every mob in the zone; you sneak past them to kill the boss. You rely on burst damage to end fights before your health runs out.
