# Wizard - Class Design Document

## 1. Class Fantasy: "The Arcane Destroyer"
The Wizard is the master of raw elemental power. They do not heal, they do not tank, they do not summon pets. They simply delete enemies from existence. In a Solo Server environment, they are the **Kiting Specialists**.

*   **Primary Stat:** **Intelligence (INT)**
*   **Class Mastery:** **"Arcane Power"**
    *   **Destructive Force:** Intelligence increases **Spell Critical Chance** and **Critical Multiplier**.
    *   **Mana Flow:** Intelligence increases **Mana Regeneration** and the potency of "Harvest" effects.

---

## 2. Combat Profile

### A. Dealing Damage (The Nuke)
Wizards deal the highest single-target magic damage in the game.
*   **Primary Source:** **Direct Damage (Nukes)** (Fire, Ice, Magic).
*   **Secondary Source:** **Point Blank AOE (PBAOE)** and **Targeted AOE**.
*   **Mechanic:** **"Mana Burn"**. A massive nuke that consumes all mana to deal catastrophic damage (Boss Finisher).

### B. Taking Damage (Glass Cannon)
Wizards wear Cloth and have the lowest HP in the game.
*   **Mitigation:** **Mana Shield**. Wizards can toggle a shield that absorbs damage at the cost of Mana. This is a desperate trade-off: survive the hit, but lose the fuel needed to kill the enemy.
*   **Survival:** **Runes** (Standard Absorb Shields) and **Stuns**.
*   **Escape:** **Teleport / Gate**. If things go wrong, you vanish to a safe spot.

---

## 3. The Toolkit

### A. Damage Tools
1.  **Ice Comet / Sunstrike:** Massive single-target nukes.
2.  **Pillar of Fire:** Targeted AOE nuke (hits up to 4 targets). Essential for "Quad Kiting".
3.  **Supernova:** PBAOE Stun + Damage.
4.  **Harvest:** Restores a large chunk of mana instantly.

### B. Mitigation/Control Tools
1.  **Root:** Immobilizes the target. (Root-Nuke-Root strategy).
2.  **Snare:** Reduces movement speed. (Essential for Kiting).
3.  **Stun:** Interrupts casting and stops movement briefly.
4.  **Mana Shield:** Toggled ability. Converts incoming damage into Mana loss. If Mana hits 0, the shield breaks and the Wizard takes full damage.

### C. Utility
1.  **Teleportation:** Can travel to any zone in the game instantly. (The ultimate travel convenience).
2.  **Evacuate:** Teleports the Wizard to a safe spot in the current zone (Panic Button).
3.  **Eye of Zomm:** Summon a scouting eye to check for named mobs safely.

### D. Area Effect (AE) Potential
*   **Quad Kiting:** The Wizard's signature solo move. Snare 4 mobs, group them up, and hit them all with "Pillar" spells while running in circles.
*   **Rain Spells:** Deals damage over 3 waves to an area.

---

## 4. Solo Strategy
*   **Level 1-30:** Root and Nuke.
*   **Level 30-60:** **Quad Kiting**. Gather 4 mobs. Snare them. Run in a circle. Cast AOE nukes until they drop. High risk, massive XP reward.
*   **Level 70+:** **Burn Strategy**. Use "Manaburn" to instantly delete named mobs, then Harvest/Meditate to recover.
