# Magician - Class Design Document

## 1. Class Fantasy: "The Master Summoner"
The Magician commands the elements. They do not fight alone; they summon powerful elemental constructs to do their bidding. In a Solo Server environment, they are the **Pet Tanks**. They bring their own tank, healer, and DPS to the fight.

*   **Primary Stat:** **Intelligence (INT)**
*   **Class Mastery:** **"Elemental Mastery"**
    *   **Construct Power:** Intelligence scales **Pet Stats** (AC, HP, STR) and **Pet Level**.
    *   **Summoned Efficiency:** Summoned items (food, water, armor) have better stats based on INT.

---

## 2. Combat Profile

### A. Dealing Damage (The Elementalist)
Magicians deal damage through their pets and nukes.
*   **Primary Source:** **Elemental Pet**.
*   **Secondary Source:** **Magic/Fire Nukes**.
*   **Mechanic:** **"Damage Shield"**. Magicians cast shields on their pets (and themselves) that damage anyone who hits them.

### B. Taking Damage (The Pet Wall)
The Magician should never take damage. The Pet takes it all.
*   **Mitigation:** Cloth Armor (Weak).
*   **Survival:** **Earth Pet**. The Earth Elemental is a tank with high HP and Root.
*   **Panic Button:** **Gate**. Or **Pet Heal**.

---

## 3. The Toolkit

### A. Damage Tools
1.  **Bolt of Fire:** Long-range nuke.
2.  **Shock of Swords:** Summons flying swords to strike the enemy.
3.  **Malaise:** Reduces enemy magic resistance (makes nukes hit harder).
4.  **Burnout:** Massive buff to Pet Strength and Haste.

### B. The Pets
1.  **Earth Elemental:** High HP, High AC, Roots target. (The Tank).
2.  **Water Elemental:** High DPS, Backstabs, Ice Nukes. (The DPS).
3.  **Air Elemental:** Stuns, Invisible. (The CC/PvP pet).
4.  **Fire Elemental:** Ranged Nuker, Damage Shield. (The Glass Cannon).

### C. Utility
1.  **Summon Food/Water:** Never go hungry.
2.  **Summon Armor/Weapons:** Equip your pet (and yourself) with magical gear.
3.  **Call of the Hero:** (Modified for Solo) -> **"Call of the Elements"**. Summons a temporary swarm of elementals to overwhelm the target.
4.  **Modulation:** Sacrifices HP to restore Mana.

### D. Area Effect (AE) Potential
*   **Rain of Fire:** Area damage over time.
*   **Beam of Quiescence:** Beam attack that hits multiple enemies.

---

## 4. Solo Strategy
*   **Level 1-30:** Send Pet. Nuke. Reclaim Pet to save mana.
*   **Level 30-60:** **Chain Summoning**. If a pet dies, summon another one instantly. The enemy has to kill an army to get to you.
*   **Level 70+:** **Pet Tanking**. Your Earth Pet is as strong as a Warrior player. You just heal it and nuke.

## 6. Summary
The Magician is the **Army of One**. You are never truly solo. You have a loyal guardian who hits like a truck and takes a beating. You support them with buffs and nukes.
