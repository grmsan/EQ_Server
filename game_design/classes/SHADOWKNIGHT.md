# Shadowknight - Class Design Document

## 1. Class Fantasy: "The Soul Eater"
The Shadowknight is the unholy union of Warrior and Necromancer. They do not protect others out of altruism; they protect themselves by draining the life force of their enemies. In a Solo Server environment, they are the **Drain Tanks**. They don't mitigate damage as well as a Warrior, but they heal it back instantly with every strike.

*   **Primary Stat:** **Intelligence (INT)**
*   **Class Mastery:** **"Soul Eater"**
    *   **Vampiric Touch:** Intelligence scales **Lifetap Efficiency**. (You heal for *more* than the damage you deal).
    *   **Unholy Aura:** Harm Touch cooldown is reduced by INT (via Cooldown Reduction) and deals bonus damage based on missing HP.

---

## 2. Combat Profile

### A. Dealing Damage (The Reaver)
Shadowknights are aggressive tanks.
*   **Primary Source:** **2H Weapons** + **Lifetap Nukes**.
*   **Secondary Source:** **DoTs** (Disease/Poison) and **Pet** (Skeleton).
*   **Mechanic:** **"Terror"**. SK spells instill fear in the target. The "Terror" line of spells applies a short-duration debuff that reduces the target's **Attack Power** (Damage Dealt) by a significant amount, effectively acting as a "Mini-Defensive" cooldown.

### B. Taking Damage (Drain Tanking)
Shadowknights wear Plate, but their true defense is offense.
*   **Mitigation:** High AC (Plate).
*   **Survival:** **Lifetaps**. Instead of preventing damage, the SK heals it back. A high-INT SK can heal for 2000+ HP every few seconds.
*   **Panic Button:** **Harm Touch**. A massive nuke that can end a fight instantly. **Feign Death**. If the fight is lost, the SK simply leaves.

---

## 3. The Toolkit

### A. Damage Tools
1.  **Lifetap (Spell):** Deals damage and heals the SK. The bread and butter.
2.  **Harm Touch (Ability):** Massive unresistible damage. (The "I Win" button).
3.  **Spear of Disease (Nuke):** Fast casting disease nuke.
4.  **Summon Dead (Pet):** A skeleton pet that adds DPS and can Bash (interrupt).

### B. Mitigation Tools
1.  **Stance: Blood (Disc):** Increases Lifetap potency but reduces AC.
2.  **Stance: Shield (Disc):** Increases AC but reduces Damage.
3.  **Feign Death:** Instantly drops all aggro. (Essential for splitting pulls).

### C. Utility
1.  **Snare (Darkness):** Reduces enemy movement speed. (Essential for Fear Kiting).
2.  **Fear:** Causes the enemy to run away. Combined with Snare, this allows the SK to kill without taking damage ("Fear Kiting").
3.  **Invisibility to Undead:** Useful in crypts/tombs.

### D. Area Effect (AE) Potential
*   **Unholy Cleave:** Passive chance for weapon attacks to cleave, striking multiple enemies in front of the Shadowknight.
*   **Vengeful Guard:** A defensive stance where successful Parries and Ripostes trigger an immediate counter-attack against all nearby enemies. This allows the SK to "reflect" damage back at a swarm of attackers.

---

## 4. Solo Strategy
*   **Level 1-30:** Face tank with Lifetaps.
*   **Level 30-60:** **Fear Kiting**. Cast Darkness (Snare), Cast Fear, chase the mob and hit it in the back. You take 0 damage.
*   **Level 70+:** **Drain Tanking**. Pull a named mob. Face tank it. Your Lifetaps heal you for more than the boss hits. You are an immortal engine of destruction.

## 6. Summary
The Shadowknight is the **Aggressive Soloist**. You control the fight with Snare and Fear. You survive by stealing life. You have the best "Get Out of Jail Free" card in the game (Feign Death).
